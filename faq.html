01 / Getting started

What is Northstar?

Northstar is a simple, visual expense tracker for understanding your spending without making personal finance feel like a second job.

Do I need an account?

You can explore the public pages without one. An account is required to enter the expense dashboard, so your private workspace is behind a sign-in.

02 / Privacy & access

Where is my data stored?

The current tracker stores expense data locally in your browser. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. For a production sync experience, the next step is connecting expenses to a Supabase database with row-level security.

Can I use Google to sign in?

Yes. The sign-in screen includes Google OAuth once the Google provider is enabled in your Supabase dashboard.

03 / Daily habits

How do I get the most from it?

Capture expenses close to the moment they happen, set a realistic monthly budget, and look for one small pattern instead of trying to optimize everything at once.

Can I export my data?

Yes. The dashboard can export your ledger as a CSV file at any time.
